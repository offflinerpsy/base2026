FAQ

Boundaries before conclusions

What does Base2026 Evidence Search cover?

It searches public records admitted to the current Base2026 public-source video evidence index. Coverage is bounded, not universal or real time, and a missing result is not evidence that no relevant source exists elsewhere.

Does no result mean the topic has no evidence?

No. It means the current query did not match an admitted public Base2026 record. Try a shorter phrase or a related term, then continue research outside Base2026 when the corpus does not cover the question.

Are creator recommendations verified or endorsed by Base2026?

No. Base2026 preserves attribution and an inspection path. A record can show that a creator made a recommendation; it does not prove the recommendation is correct, effective or suitable for your case.

Do several matching records mean experts agree?

No. Several records may repeat one origin, rely on the same assumption or discuss different claims. Record count, creator count and source independence are separate questions.

Can I search all of TikTok or read full transcripts here?

No. TikTok is an original-source type inside the admitted corpus. Base2026 is not a TikTok-wide search engine and does not publish raw media, raw captions or full transcripts.

Is the tool free, and do I need an API key?

The public search is free to use and has no paywall. The browser search does not require an API key. Its public endpoint is read-only and does not expose write, moderation, credential or private-data functions.
