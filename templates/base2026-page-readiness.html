What can this check tell me?

Title and H1 elements, robots meta declarations, canonical syntax, ordinary HTTP(S) anchors and JSON-LD parseability. It does not validate schema meaning, rendered visibility, linked pages or search results. Missing structured data is not automatically a problem.
