    How the builder works

    Keep every note close to its source.

    1. 01

      Frame one question

      Choose the audience and a brief, memo or outline format. The builder records your framing but does not answer it for you.

    2. 02

      Resolve selected IDs

      Each exact public record or source ID is sent to the existing read-only get_source contract. At most eight lookups run, with bounded public metadata and short excerpts.

    3. 03

      Inspect before reuse

      Open the Base2026 record, creator link and original source where available. Unknown links, unavailable records and missing excerpts remain explicit in the preview and exports.

    What it is

    A local reading scaffold.

    • A deterministic way to organize up to eight already-selected public records.
    • A visible path from record ID to creator, original source and bounded public excerpts.
    • A portable Markdown or JSON note for your own review workflow.

    What it is not

    A truth or consensus engine.

    • Not a fact-check, ranking, quality, effectiveness or independence verdict.
    • Not an external crawl, web-wide search, recommendation or citation measurement.
    • Not an LLM answer, private-data workflow, raw-transcript or media surface.

    FAQ

    Boundaries before conclusions

    How many records can I use?

    The builder accepts at most eight unique canonical Base2026 record or source IDs. Extra, malformed or arbitrary items are rejected and shown as input unknowns instead of being silently added.

    What does the preview actually contain?

    It contains your question, audience and format, exact selected record IDs, safe public attribution, available creator and original-source links, and up to three short public excerpts per resolved record. It does not synthesize a conclusion.

    What happens when a record cannot be resolved?

    The submitted ID remains in the preview and exports with an unresolved status and reason. The builder does not invent a title, creator, source link or excerpt for it.

    Does a brief prove that the records agree?

    No. The tool does not infer truth, consensus or independence. Repeated wording, creators or source URLs need separate human inspection and do not become a score.

    Does this save my question or source IDs?

    No. Lookups are read-only public MCP requests, and Markdown or JSON exports are created in your browser. This page does not use GET/query prefill and its controls do not serialize your framing into a URL. It has no D1 write, account requirement, private binding or LLM call.
